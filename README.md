🛒 Superstore Sales & Customer Analysis

📌 Overview

An end-to-end Business Data Analysis project built using SQL Server to analyze Superstore transactional data and uncover insights across sales, profitability, products, customers, time trends, and shipping performance.

The project focuses on moving beyond basic querying to answer:

> What happened? Why did it happen? And what should the business do about it?



The final analysis and findings were documented in a Jupyter Notebook report.

💡 Key Business Insights

🔹 Technology was the strongest profit driver, contributing approximately 50.8% of total profit.

🔹 Phones ranked #1 in Sub-Category Sales and contributed around 15.5% of total profit.

🔹 Tables revealed a major Sales–Profit mismatch, generating high revenue while contributing approximately -6.2% of total profit.

🔹 Furniture ranked high in Sales but showed weak profitability, with discounting playing an important role.

🔹 Sales followed a clear seasonal pattern, increasing throughout the year and reaching their highest levels in Q4.

🔹 Single-purchase customers represent an opportunity for re-engagement and retention campaigns.

🚀 Business Recommendations

💰 Improve Profitability

Reassess discount strategies for loss-making Sub-Categories.

Review unprofitable products at the individual SKU level.

Focus on improving margin rather than revenue alone.


📦 Prepare for Peak Season

Increase inventory and operational capacity ahead of Q4.

Align supply-chain planning with seasonal demand.


🚀 Grow High-Value Categories

Increase focus and investment in high-profit Technology products.

Use regional performance to support targeted marketing.


👥 Improve Customer Retention

Use different strategies for different RFM segments:

Champions → Rewards & VIP programs
Potential Loyalists → Cross-selling & Upselling
At Risk → Win-back campaigns
Hibernating → Reactivation campaigns
